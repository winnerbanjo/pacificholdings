Our Projects

Power Generation (Pacific Energy Company Ltd)
Pacific Energy owns and operates two of Nigeria’s most important power plants:
• Omotosho Power Plant (Ondo State) – 335 MW installed capacity

Olorunsogo Power Plant (Ogun State) – 335 MW installed capacity
Both plants are among the best-performing Independent Power Producer (IPP) facilities in the country.
